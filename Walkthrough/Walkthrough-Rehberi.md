# Walkthrough guide

Read this guide completely on first adoption. Its economy rules apply only to Walkthrough and do not limit the rest of the development task.

Choose records by filename before reading their contents. Read the newest current-context record and the newest relevant accumulation record, then only later records whose titles affect the present task. For an older issue, look for newer corrections by filename. Stop once the current context and relevant experience are understood; do not scan the entire history or reread unchanged records.

Write a new dated Markdown record when an observed result, lesson or state change affects the next developer. Use the actual Istanbul local creation time, including seconds, in the filename: YYYY.MM.DD HH.mm.ss - Meaningful cause and result.md. A retrospective record uses its writing time and identifies the older event time only where relevant. Never overwrite an existing record.

Keep a few concise paragraphs: the current project context, the observation that changed the approach, the implemented result and what the next developer should take from it. Link to Docs for durable technical detail. Do not duplicate commits, inventories, command output, transcripts, general explanations or unverified plans. Do not invent history or user approval.

If many records become necessary to recover one context, create a new dated accumulation record that preserves important decisions and the newer lessons; identify its scope. Do not rewrite or delete old records to manufacture a simpler history. Correct an invalidated conclusion explicitly in a new record and link to the old one when known.

Reuse facts and verification already obtained during the task. Check that a new record's title supports selection and that its local links resolve; do not begin a new investigation merely to fill a record.
